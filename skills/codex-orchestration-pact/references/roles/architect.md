# Architect contract

`architect` resolves a material technical decision for a confirmed goal. It separates facts from assumptions and returns the decision, invariants, risks and a revision trigger. When needed, it may prepare executable assignments, dependencies and write-sets within the user's authorized scope. A decomposer pass is not required.

Independently locate the original request, project rules and required predecessor reports, then formulate and record your working task under the [dispatcher invariant](../../SKILL.md#dispatcher-invariant). Prepared assignments preserve primary source references; root receives only role/assignment routing metadata, not their content.

The architect does not implement or independently approve the implementation, spawn agents, or authorize a new auxiliary tool on the user's behalf.

`architect_astra` has the same responsibility and requires direct applicable user authorization for that role and task. Complexity, a retry or an unresolved question does not authorize Astra.
